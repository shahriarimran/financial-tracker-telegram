# Signal methodology

The workbook is the single source of truth for Gold, Silver, and USD signals. Its formulas and Power Query inputs determine `BUY`, `SELL`, or `HOLD`; scheduler and VBScript layers only invoke `DailyAutoRun`.

The public-release work does not change thresholds, formulas, query logic, dashboard design, or investment methodology. Validate formulas and source availability locally before relying on a signal. Signals are educational information, not investment advice.
